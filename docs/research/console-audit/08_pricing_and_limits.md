# 08 — Pricing, plans & limits `[OBSERVED]`

## Plans (Billing & plan page + marketing pricing page)

| | Starter | Business |
|---|---|---|
| Price | **$23/mo** (~KES 2,990) | **$69/mo** (~KES 8,970) |
| Users | 3 included, then ~$5.4/user/mo | 10 included, then ~$3.85/user/mo |
| Projects | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Includes | Daily logs, checklists, schedule, photos; free client accounts; quotes, invoices, change orders; inspections, punch lists & safety | Everything in Starter + Financials, BOQ & payment certificates, Inventory/equipment/subcontractors, Reports + role-based access for whole team, Onboarding & priority support |
| Yearly | — | **$690/yr** (two months free) |

- Billing via **Paystack**: "M-Pesa, card & bank · cancel anytime. Prices billed in USD." Currency display toggle KES/USD is presentation-only.
- "Your team: 1 user → 23/month" — seat pricing scales with team size.
- Trial: **7 days free** on signup; our account showed "4 days left".
- Client accounts: **free** for the contractor's customers.
- **AI credits**: separate metered billing (`/ai-credits`, checkout/verify) — AI features consume credits [INFERRED].
- **Self-hosted option**: "In Our Cloud, or On Your Own Servers" — custom install, custom features/workflows/branding, integrations; contact sales.

## Feature gating [INFERRED]
Starter lacks: financials/BOQ/certificates, inventory/equipment/subcontractors, full-team reports/RBAC. Inspections/punch/safety ARE in Starter. AI credits likely available on both tiers (metered).

## Limits observed
- Daily-log video ≤ 20MB (~30–60s).
- Inspection request: readiness photos 0/10, evidence photos 0/10, videos 0/3.
- Team size drives price (per-user overage).
