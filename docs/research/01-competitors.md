  - Earned Value Management (EVM): Cost Performance Index (CPI) and Schedule Performance Index (SPI) derived from live BOQ progress [Tag: `VENDOR CLAIM` - [lobocon.co/earned-value](https://lobocon.co/earned-value)]
  - Cash flow forecasting and project cash position tracking [Tag: `VENDOR CLAIM` - [lobocon.co/cash-flow](https://lobocon.co/cash-flow)]
  - Execution scheduling: MS Project schedule import, CPM (Critical Path Method) tracking [Tag: `VENDOR CLAIM` - [lobocon.co/scheduling](https://lobocon.co/scheduling)]
  - Planned vs. Actual: Weekly certified site measurement prior to billing [Tag: `VENDOR CLAIM` - [lobocon.co/planned-vs-actual](https://lobocon.co/planned-vs-actual)]
  - Resource tracking: Labor, materials, fuel, subcontractors, and plant equipment [Tag: `VENDOR CLAIM` - [lobocon.co/resources](https://lobocon.co/resources)]
  - Contract administration: Timestamped RFIs ([lobocon.co/rfis](https://lobocon.co/rfis)) and drawing revision control with receipts ([lobocon.co/documents](https://lobocon.co/documents)) [Tag: `VENDOR CLAIM`]
- **Pricing & Contract Terms (Exact Quoted Terms):**
  - **Pilot:** **KES 300,000 for a 90-day pilot** covering one live project with unlimited users, including up to 40 hours of BOQ and schedule migration, fully credited against year one if adopted [Tag: `VERIFIED` - [lobocon.co/pilot](https://lobocon.co/pilot)]. Target profile stated explicitly: *"For contractors running more than one live contract."*
  - **Post-Pilot Annual Subscription:** Quoted on pilot page as **KES 100,000 per month**, or **KES 1,038,000 per year billed in advance** (which equals **KES 86,500 per month** effective rate), **per project**, with unlimited users, excl. VAT. Renewals capped at 10% per year [Tag: `VERIFIED` - [lobocon.co/pilot](https://lobocon.co/pilot)].
- **Roles:** Quantity Surveyor (QS), Project Manager (PM), Client / Developer, Consulting Engineer.
- **Client Portal:** Collaborative dashboard providing visibility across stakeholders ("same numbers for the QS, the PM, the client and the Engineer") [Tag: `VENDOR CLAIM` - [lobocon.co/collaboration](https://lobocon.co/collaboration)].
- **Offline Capability:** No offline capability stated or advertised on live site; operates as a web-based platform [Tag: `UNVERIFIED`].
- **M-Pesa Integration:** None stated on live site; contracts and cash flows operate through commercial bank/invoicing channels [Tag: `VERIFIED`].

### 1.2 Strukchad (Strukchad Ltd)
- **Live URL:** [strukchad.com](https://strukchad.com) | Pricing: [strukchad.com/pricing](https://strukchad.com/pricing) | Integrations: [strukchad.com/integrations](https://strukchad.com/integrations) | Access Date: 2026-10-27 | Tag: `VERIFIED`
- **Product Overview:** Operating platform for contractors in Kenya to connect field reporting (attendance, material deliveries) with back-office project accounting.
- **Features:**
  - Labor tracking: Attendance, timesheets, and site crew activity by project [Tag: `VENDOR CLAIM` - [strukchad.com](https://strukchad.com)]
  - Material control: Requisitions, delivery notes, stock movement, and consumption logging [Tag: `VENDOR CLAIM` - [strukchad.com](https://strukchad.com)]
  - Site-to-office reporting: Mobile foreman app linking site updates to back-office project accounting [Tag: `VENDOR CLAIM` - [strukchad.com](https://strukchad.com)]
  - AI insights and automated financial report generation [Tag: `VENDOR CLAIM` - [strukchad.com/pricing](https://strukchad.com/pricing)]
- **Pricing & Seat Structure (Exact Quoted Terms):**
  - All prices quoted in KES, exclusive of VAT [Tag: `VERIFIED` - [strukchad.com/pricing](https://strukchad.com/pricing)]:
    - **Free Trial:** 14 days, KES 0, up to 5 active projects, 5 user accounts.
    - **Lite:** **KES 2,000 per user / month** (mobile-only access for field officers: Foremen, Storekeepers, Site Managers; no back-office access; unlimited projects).
    - **Professional:** **KES 5,000 per user / month** (for Owners, Directors, PMs, Finance, Procurement; unlimited projects, AI insights, advanced reporting, payment integrations).
    - **Custom:** Tailored enterprise deployment, on-premise option, SLA.
    - **Property Management License:** KES 3,000 / month (first 2 users) + KES 1,500 / extra user / month.
  - *Note on Seat Model:* The pricing page mentions "Unlimited user accounts" as a bullet alongside per-user pricing. In practice, the platform allows creating user records but charges per billable seat, recommending a mixed deployment of Lite seats on site and Professional seats in the office [Tag: `VERIFIED` - [strukchad.com/pricing](https://strukchad.com/pricing)].
- **Roles:** Foremen, Storekeepers, Site Managers, Owners / Directors, PMs, Finance, Procurement.
- **Client Portal:** Mentions developer workflow, but does not offer a standalone dedicated client portal [Tag: `VENDOR CLAIM`].
- **Offline Capability:** Not explicitly stated on live site; mobile experience delivered via responsive web/foreman app [Tag: `UNVERIFIED`].
- **M-Pesa Integration:** Live integration page explicitly lists support for M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Pesalink, and bank statement reconciliations [Tag: `VERIFIED` - [strukchad.com/integrations](https://strukchad.com/integrations)].
